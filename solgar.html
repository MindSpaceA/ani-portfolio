About the project

A modern wellness eCommerce experience.

The goal was to create a premium and trustworthy wellness store experience for Solgar products, combining clean editorial design with practical eCommerce functionality.

What I worked on

I customized the Shopify storefront and created custom sections and layouts to present wellness products in a clear and premium way.

The interface was designed to feel clean, modern and responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
